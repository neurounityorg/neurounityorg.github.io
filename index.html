01 / Working prototype

How quickly can you respond to a visual signal?

What the timer includes: visual detection, attention, decision time, nerve signaling, and movement.

Where your data goesYour five results stay in this tab. Reloading clears them, and the task does not upload them.

What the score meansThis is not a health test. The score does not measure intelligence, athletic ability, or a medical condition.

02 / How it works

What each subject adds to the experiment

A reaction-time score is only a starting point. Each subject helps explain part of the result and shows what the experiment cannot prove.

P

Physics

Follow the signal

Reaction time includes electrical signaling, synaptic transmission, and muscle activation. Students mark where delays can enter the system.

M

Mathematics

Look past one number

Students compare medians, ranges, and repeated trials instead of treating one fast click as a conclusion.

C

Computer science

Turn a question into code

JavaScript controls the delay, timestamps each click, and keeps the five results in the current tab.

signal → timestamp

+

Medicine

Set limits on the result

Short bursts of stress are not always harmful, but long-term stress can affect health. This activity measures a response to one visual signal. It cannot explain why one student was faster or slower.
